Welcome

M4Brew converts messy audiobook folders into clean, chapterised M4B files — ready for Plex, Jellyfin, or Audiobookshelf.

Folder structure (important)

Audiobooks/
└── Author Name/
    └── Book Title/
        └── audio files

M4Brew needs this layout to name files correctly and generate chapter titles.

Supported input formats

  • MP3 (single or multiple files)
  • M4A (single or multiple files)
  • Existing M4B (merged into one)

Always outputs a single, chapterised .m4b file.

Nothing gets deleted

  • Your originals are never touched automatically
  • Converted files sit alongside your existing folders
  • Cleanup is always a separate, deliberate step
  • Cancel anytime — no half-written files left behind
